À la carte

Or book a single service.

Useful when you know exactly what you need — a shopping day before a trip, one event look, a packing session the night before a flight.

01

Wardrobe Edit & Organisation

The foundation of everything else. Everything comes out, everything borderline gets tried on, and what stays goes back in a system you can actually maintain — matching hangers, category zones, colour runs, shelf labels. I leave you with a one-page map of where things live so it survives the housekeeper, the laundry cycle and a busy week.

Half day AED 1,800  ·  Full day AED 3,200
02

Personal Styling & Colour

A studio or in-home session on proportion, silhouette and colour. We establish the shapes that work on your frame, the palette that works against your skin in UAE daylight, and a repeatable formula for work, weekend and evening. You leave with a written style brief you can shop from for years.

Session AED 2,400  ·  With lookbook AED 3,600
03

Personal Shopping

I scout first and shop second. Fitting rooms are held, sizes are pulled and the route is planned before you arrive — Dubai Mall, Mall of the Emirates, City Walk, Galleria on Al Wasl, or the independent studios in Alserkal and Al Quoz. No commission is taken from any retailer.

Half day AED 2,800  ·  Full day AED 4,600
04

Relocation Wardrobe Reset

For families landing in the UAE. We unpack, work out what survives 40°C and what belongs in storage, brief you on the dress codes that actually matter here — office, government offices, majlis, brunch, desert — and rebuild the wardrobe around them. Usually done inside the first fortnight.

Two days from AED 4,500
05

Executive Presence

Built for founders, partners and anyone regularly on a stage or a camera. A tight, repeatable uniform with defined board-day, keynote and client-dinner looks, tailoring supervised end-to-end, and a packing template that survives a four-city travel week in one carry-on.

From AED 6,500
06

Events & Bridal

One night that has to be right. Sourcing across UAE boutiques and regional designers, fittings coordinated, tailoring liaised, and a look sheet down to the shoe, the bag and the jewellery. Wedding parties and multi-day celebrations quoted individually.

Single look from AED 5,000
07

Capsule & Travel Packing

A 30-piece capsule for a season, or a packed suitcase for a specific trip with every outfit photographed so you can rebuild it on the road. Popular before summer in Europe and before Ramadan and Eid.

From AED 1,500
08

Gift Vouchers

Any service can be given as a voucher, sent as a printed card or a digital note. Valid twelve months, transferable, and the recipient chooses how to spend it.

Any value from AED 1,500
Always included

The parts nobody quotes for.

Travel anywhere within Dubai. The discovery call. A written summary after every session. Removal of donation bags to a UAE charity of your choosing, and the consignment paperwork if you'd rather sell.

Also included: the tailor run. Alterations are where most wardrobes are quietly won, and chasing a tailor in Satwa is not a good use of your week — so I do it, and I check the finished work before it comes back to you.

Not included: anything you buy, alteration charges, consignment commission, and travel outside Dubai (quoted at cost).
