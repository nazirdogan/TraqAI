Why now

Twelve wardrobes' worth of clothes. Nothing to wear.

Dubai asks a lot of a wardrobe. You need desk-to-dinner in 42°C, something for a majlis and something for a beach club, abaya-appropriate layering and a coat that only leaves the house twice a year for London. Most people solve this by buying more. It rarely helps.

A wardrobe edit works the other way around. We start with what you own, establish what actually fits your body and your calendar today, and remove the friction — the pieces that don't fit, the trends that never suited you, the four near-identical white shirts you keep re-buying because you can't find the good one.

What's left gets organised so that getting dressed is a two-second decision, not a twenty-minute negotiation with yourself.

The process

Five steps. Usually two days.

Nothing leaves your home without your say-so, and nothing gets bought before we know what's missing.

01

Discovery call

Twenty minutes, free. Your week, your work, your climate problems, what you keep reaching for and what you keep avoiding.

02

The audit

I come to you. Everything comes out. We try on the maybes — because a wardrobe decision made on a hanger is usually the wrong one.

03

The edit

Keep, tailor, consign, donate. I handle the tailor drop-off and the consignment paperwork; donations go to a UAE charity of your choosing.

04

The rebuild

Everything returns by category, colour and occasion. Matching hangers, labelled shelves, a system your housekeeper can maintain in five minutes.

05

The lookbook

A digital lookbook of outfits shot on your own pieces, plus a prioritised shopping list with sizes, budget and where in Dubai to find it.
