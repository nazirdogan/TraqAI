How a session runs

The part after the session is what makes it stick.

Before

A short survey of the people attending, a call with you, and a look at how a few real tasks get done today. The agenda is built from that.

During

Hands on your own tasks

People bring live work and leave having done it faster, with the guardrails explained as we go rather than on a policy slide at the end.

Questions about AI training

Is this a course people do on their own?

No. It’s live, hands-on training for your team, built around your tools and your work. People leave having done real tasks, not watched a demo.

Which tools do you train on?

The ones your team already has: Claude, ChatGPT, Microsoft 365 Copilot and Gemini. No new platform to buy.

How big does the team need to be?

Sessions run from a handful of people to a whole company. Workshops suit 4 to 25 people; larger rollouts run team by team.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the format and the number of people. You’ll know the scope and cost up front, in a written proposal.

Where do you work?

In person or remote. We work with teams in the UAE and worldwide, and the work runs the same way wherever you are.
