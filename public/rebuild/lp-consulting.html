Questions about AI consulting

What do I get at the end?

A plan grounded in your real tools and workflows: where AI saves the most time, what to do first, which tools to use, and the guardrails to use them safely. If you go on to build, you get working systems your team owns.

How is this different from an AI agency?

An agency builds for you and keeps the know-how. We work alongside your team and leave the capability with your people, so you aren’t locked into fees to keep things running.

Do we need to hire a Head of AI?

Usually not yet. Most growing businesses don’t have a full year of full-time AI work. An embedded partner gives you the same senior direction, part-time, until you do.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your team and what you want to achieve. Most clients start with the fixed-scope audit, so you see the value before any longer commitment, and you’ll know the scope and cost up front.

Where do you work?

In person or remote. We work with teams in the UAE and worldwide, and the work runs the same way wherever you are.
