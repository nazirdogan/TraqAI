The Transformation

One clear direction for your business.

We find what’s holding your business back, make sure your foundations can carry AI, build it into the tools you already use, and hand it over so your team runs it.

Get in touchSee the four chapters

Built for businesses that have outgrown the way they run

Growth means more admin, not more output.

The day is held together by spreadsheets, inboxes and good people working late.

You want AI working inside the business, not another tool to manage.

The four chapters

It moves at the pace your business needs. Every chapter ends with something you can hold, and you decide whether to carry on.

01

Audit and mapping

We sit with how work really moves through your business: where it slows, where it leaks, and where people wait on each other. We talk to the people doing the work, not just the people managing it.

What happens

  • Interviews with the people who do the work
  • A walk through your tools, inboxes and spreadsheets
  • Every bottleneck costed in time and money

What we need from you

  • An hour or two from a few of your people
  • Access to look at how things run today
You leave withA bottleneck map. Where time and money leak, ranked by what AI can fix first.
02

Feasibility

We look under the floorboards. Is your data clean enough, and are your systems ready to carry AI? Where they aren’t, we fix that first, as part of the work, so nothing gives way later.

What happens

  • A check of your data, systems and access
  • Data cleanup where it’s needed, scoped with you
  • A go or no-go for every idea on the map

What we need from you

  • Read access to the systems in scope
  • Someone who knows where the data lives
You leave withA readiness report. What’s ready, what needs cleaning, and a clear go or no-go for each idea.
03

Implementation

We build AI into the tools and workflows you already run, including agents that handle work on their own. No new platform for your team to learn, and a person stays in the loop wherever it matters.

What happens

  • Automations and agents built into your own tools
  • Approval steps wherever a person should decide
  • Tested on real work before anyone relies on it

What we need from you

  • A named owner on your side
  • Feedback from the people who use it
You leave withA working build, running in your own tools.
04

Ownership

We hand it over properly. Documentation, access, and the people who run it shown how, so it keeps working without us. Then we step back.

What happens

  • Documentation written for your team, not for us
  • Admin access and accounts in your name
  • Your operators shown how to run and adjust it

What we need from you

  • The people who will own it day to day
  • An afternoon for the handover
You leave withA runbook and a clean handover. Everything we built is yours.

Not ready for all four? Start with the audit.

You get the bottleneck map on its own, and you decide what happens next. Plenty of clients start here.

Questions about the Transformation

How much does it cost?

It depends on what the audit finds. You’ll know the scope and the cost before anything gets built, and the audit stands on its own if you stop there.

How long does it take?

As long as your business needs. A focused fix can move quickly, and a wider change takes longer. We agree the scope with you up front, so there are no surprises.

What if our data is a mess?

Most is. Cleaning it up is part of Feasibility, scoped in or out with you, so nothing is built on foundations that won’t hold.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Accounts, access and documentation are in your name from the start, and the handover makes sure your team can run it without us.

Where do you work?

Everywhere. We work with teams globally, in person or remotely.
