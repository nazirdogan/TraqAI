About Traq Collective

Calm, capable businesses. That’s the whole job.

Traq is a founder-led AI consultancy. We find what’s holding a business back, use AI to take it off the team’s hands, and leave the capability behind when we go.

Hi, I’m Nazir. I build AI systems and train teams to use them every day, including a full operation I run end to end.

Traq exists because most businesses don’t need more AI tools. They need someone to sit with how the work actually happens, find what’s getting in the way, and fix it properly, with AI where it earns its place and not where it doesn’t.

When you work with Traq, you work with me, from the first conversation to the handover. No junior team, no deck you’ll forget. We build it, we show your people how to run it, and then we step back.

We work with teams globally, in person or remotely.
