The Hard Truth

Your AI agency is already dead. Everyone and their mum started one in the last 18 months — and businesses aren't impressed anymore. When you sell build, you compete on deliverables. When you sell knowledge, you compete on expertise. The highest-ticket clients don't want someone to build it and leave. They want someone to sit with them, understand their business, and show them exactly how AI fits. That commands a completely different price.
