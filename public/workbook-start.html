The 5 jobs a lean founder should hand to AI first

Read this first, no input needed. The work that quietly eats a founder's week, and what AI can carry so you do not have to hire for it yet. If you only take one thing from this workbook, take these.

1

Customer replies and support

Answering the same questions by hand, at all hours, is the first thing that pulls a founder off real work.

Hand to AI: drafted replies, a smart FAQ, and first-line triage, so you only touch the messages that truly need you.

2

Content and marketing

Posts, captions, emails and a landing page are a full job on their own, and the reason most founders go quiet.

Hand to AI: a content engine you steer, drafts in your voice that you approve, not a blank page every morning.

3

Sales follow-up and outreach

Leads go cold because nobody chased them. That is revenue leaking while you build.

Hand to AI: personalised first drafts, follow-up sequences and CRM tidying, so no lead slips through.

4

Admin, ops and documents

Invoices, scheduling, notes, data entry. None of it grows the business, all of it takes your time.

Hand to AI: templated docs, meeting notes to action items, and simple automations between your tools.

5

Research and decisions

As a team of one, you have no one to think out loud with, so calls get made slowly or not at all.

Hand to AI: fast research, competitor scans, and a sounding board that pressure-tests your thinking before you commit.

The reason this works now, and did not three years ago: the gains from AI land hardest exactly where a lean founder lives, on routine knowledge work, and the biggest lift goes to the least specialised hands.
