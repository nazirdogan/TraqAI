Agencies price labour. Consultants price the outcome. Build a ladder so every prospect can say yes.

A paid audit, strategy session, or roadmap. Low commitment, fast yes.

Core (your main offer)

Your signature engagement. A fixed-scope advisory sprint or program.

Retainer

Ongoing advisory. Monthly access, reviews, and direction.
