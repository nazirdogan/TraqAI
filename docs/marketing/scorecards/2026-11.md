## 4. This month's read

**Overall picture:** Traq Collective is currently invisible across all 20 priority queries in AI answer engines. No query returns traqcollective.com in approximated citations. Even the brand query (Q4 — "What is Traq Collective?") does not surface the site; Clutch and GoodFirms directory pages appear instead, and the site is confused with Traq.ai (a US sales-intelligence tool).

**What worked:** Nothing to report yet — this is month one of monitoring. Directores (Clutch, GoodFirms) have been indexed, which is a baseline.

**What didn't:** The site itself is not being discovered for any of the 20 queries. The fractional Head of AI cluster (Q5–8), which maps directly to a Traq service, is entirely owned by fractionus.com, kore1.com, and lilachbullock.com — all with dedicated, well-structured pages targeting this exact topic.

**Three additional observations:**
1. **Dubai Chambers launched an agentic AI training programme for 14,000 member companies in September 2026.** This is a significant macro signal — it validates Traq's positioning but also raises the competitive floor. Being listed or associated with this initiative would be the fastest route to UAE AI-training query visibility.
2. **lilachbullock.com is the single biggest competitor across comparison queries (Q7, Q8, Q17).** It has a content factory producing multiple dedicated pages per topic cluster — fractional CAIO, AI consultant vs. agency, etc. Traq needs at least one strong page per cluster to compete.
3. **Query 19 (agentic AI workflows) is owned by Slack.** Deprioritise this for now; competing with Tier 1 tech brands on definitional queries is a year-plus investment.

**One highest-impact next move:** Publish a single, well-structured explainer page targeting Query 1 — "What is an AI enablement partner and what do they do?" — using the exact phrase in the H1, a clear definition block in the first 100 words, and a structured breakdown of what the role covers. This query is currently returning only job listings, meaning no editorial page owns the definition. Traq is uniquely positioned to write this authoritatively, and a definition-first page here would compound across related queries (Q2, Q3, Q7). Target URL: `/insights/what-is-an-ai-enablement-partner`. This is the single action most likely to generate first AI citations within the next 60–90 days.
