## Section 4 — This Month's Read

**Baseline month — no citations yet, but the trajectory is clear.**

**What's working (or on track to):**
- The query clusters are well-chosen. Q17 ("AI consultant vs AI agency") and Q3 ("help my team use AI tools") have weak competition from small boutique operators — these are achievable near-term citation wins, not enterprise-dominated queries.
- The existing field-notes library (14 pieces published in July 2026) shows strong content velocity. The five-hour rule article, "how to make AI usage stick after training", and "where small teams should start with AI" map directly to Q13–Q15.
- The /ai-readiness page exists and could earn citations for Q20 if properly structured with a definition-first answer block.
- The /fractional-head-of-ai page exists and could break into Q5–Q8 if it matches the phrasing competitors are using (many use "fractional Chief AI Officer" / CAIO rather than "fractional Head of AI").

**What isn't working:**
- The brand indexing error (Padel Court description) is actively blocking every citation path. This is the single largest blocker and must be fixed before any other work lands.
- No Clutch, GoodFirms, or UAE directory listings exist yet (all marked "Not started" in `off-page-checklist.md`). Clutch.co appears in results for almost every UAE query — without a profile there, Traq can't be included in the roundups that AI engines actually cite.
- The "AI enablement partner" category terminology is being interpreted by search engines as channel-partner reseller training (zinfi.com, disco.co), not as Traq's embedded consultancy model. The site needs more explicit use of the phrase "embedded AI partner" and the job-to-be-done language ("wire AI into the tools you already pay for") so search engines classify Traq correctly.
- The /insights library has only 2 full articles (vs. 14 field-notes). Field-notes are short; AI engines prefer longer, definition-rich guides that directly answer a query in the first paragraph.

**One highest-impact next move:**

> **Publish one definitive `/insights` guide: "AI Consultant vs AI Agency: Which One Does Your Business Actually Need?"**

Here's why this is the single best ROI action this month:

The competitors winning Q17 ("AI consultant vs AI agency, which do I need?") are all small boutique operators writing specific explainer articles — lilachbullock.com, shooflyai.com, meruai.co, heedaisolutions.com. None of them are large platforms. Traq can match them with one 1,200–1,800 word guide that: (a) opens with a crisp definition block answering the exact query, (b) introduces the "embedded AI partner" model as a third option between consultant and agency, (c) gives a simple decision framework (need strategy only → consultant; need execution only → agency; need both → embedded partner), and (d) ends with the free AI Readiness Assessment CTA. This query is asked by exactly the buyer Traq wants, the competition is beatable, and it creates a citation target that earns links from the same roundups Traq is trying to get into. One guide, one month, first realistic citation shot.
