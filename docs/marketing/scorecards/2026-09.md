## Section 4. This month's read

### What we found

**We are not being cited anywhere on the 20 priority queries.** The site's Google indexed description still shows legacy content ("Padel Court Pricing Analytics") on the root domain, which is a significant indexing concern — the AI consultancy repositioning has not propagated to search. This is the most urgent issue: without correct indexing, no amount of content work will produce citations.

**The competitor landscape is clear:**
- **UAE local queries (9–12):** Clutch directory listings dominate. Every UAE-based AI firm appearing in AI engine answers has a Clutch profile. Traq does not appear to.
- **Fractional Head of AI queries (5–8):** A cluster of specialist sites (headofai.ai, chiefaiofficer.com, fractionus.com, iternal.ai, kore1.com) own these queries with dedicated long-form guides. These are harder to crack short-term.
- **Comparison/how-to queries (17–20):** The easiest terrain — many ranking pages come from small independent consultants with domain authority similar to where Traq is now. This is where to invest content effort this month.
- **Brand query (Q4):** Traq.ai (a different US sales-conversation tool) dominates "Traq" searches and is likely to be cited by AI engines when anyone asks about Traq. This brand confusion is a medium-term risk.

**One high-leverage signal:** The UAE Cabinet's May 2026 approval of Agentic AI training for 80,000 federal employees and Sheikh Hamdan's plan to equip 295,000 Dubai private-sector companies represents a massive tailwind. No content in the search results (from any competitor) directly connects to this government mandate as a context for hiring an embedded AI partner. This is a first-mover opportunity.

### What didn't work

No Traq content is appearing in any priority query. The gap between Traq's service positioning and its indexed content is still wide. Newly published guides (AI Consulting Rates, AI ROI) may not yet have been crawled by Perplexity/ChatGPT browsing.

### One highest-impact next move

**Fix the indexing problem first, then publish one piece targeting Q3 + Q17/18 simultaneously.**

1. **Immediate (this week):** Verify in Google Search Console that the home page is correctly indexed with AI consultancy content. If still showing old content, request a recrawl of `/` and all key service pages. Submit the sitemap if not already done.

2. **This month's content priority:** Publish a guide titled *"Who can help my team actually use the AI tools we already pay for — and should you hire a consultant or do it yourselves?"* This one page targets queries 3, 17, and 18 simultaneously. The SERP for all three is populated by small independent consultants — not HBR or Asana. Traq can realistically rank and get cited within 60–90 days if the page is structured with a clear definition-first answer block, a comparison table (consultant vs agency vs DIY), and a UAE-specific case example. This is the single highest-impact content action available now.

3. **Off-page (this month):** Create or claim a Clutch profile. Clutch is cited in both Q9 and Q10 results. A presence there is table stakes for UAE AI consultant discovery.
